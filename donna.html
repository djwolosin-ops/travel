July 16 — Icefields Parkway Driving Day
Fuel up before you leave Baker Creek! Food and fuel are very limited on the Parkway. Start with a full tank, water, snacks, and layers.
🏔 Bow Lake
Stunning glacial lake — great early stop
🏔 Peyto Lake Viewpoint
Famous turquoise wolf-shaped lake — short uphill walk to viewpoint
🏔 Waterfowl Lakes
Beautiful valley views, great photo stop
🗻 Mistaya Canyon
Short walk to dramatic slot canyon
🧊 Columbia Icefield / Athabasca Glacier
Largest icefield in the Rockies — walk up to the glacier toe
💧 Sunwapta Falls
Short walk, dramatic double waterfall
💧 Athabasca Falls
Most powerful waterfall in the Rockies — final stop before Jasper
Plan this as a full day The Parkway is 232 km — but with all the stops it easily takes 8+ hours. Arrive in Jasper in the evening.

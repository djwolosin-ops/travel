Room Upgrade Strategies

1 –Bid for Upgrade (Best Bet)

HAL's Move Up program –expect an email 60–90 days before sailing (July/August) inviting you to bid on higher categories. You name your price above the minimum. Target categories:

Outside / Ocean ViewNatural light, no balcony

2 –Call HAL Directly

Call with confirmation 3TXC4T and ask about paid upgrades. Unsold cabins get discounted as sailing approaches – sometimes moves for a few hundred dollars.

Tel: 1-866-317-4711

3 –Online Check-In (90 Days Out)

When check-in opens (~July 12), complimentary upgrades sometimes appear if the ship is undersold in your category. Check immediately when it opens.

4 –Pier Day Ask

Ask the check-in agent at the Tokyo pier on Oct 11 if any day-of upgrades are available. Ships with unsold inventory sometimes deal on embarkation day.

Why balcony is the priority: Japan in October is stunning. Kanmon Strait transit (Oct 15) and Hakodate approach (Oct 21) are best experienced from a private balcony. That's the upgrade worth paying for.

Good to Know
Online Check-In Complete at celebritycruises.com/onlinecheckin at least 3 days before sailing. Print your Xpress Pass.
Grand Cayman is a Tender Port You'll take a small tender boat from the ship to shore. Allow extra time and watch for tender ticket times onboard.
Reef-Safe Sunscreen Avoid oxybenzone, octinoxate, and titanium dioxide — some ports ban them and it's better for the reef.
Evening Chic Nights 1–2 formal nights onboard. Check the daily program in your stateroom. Smart Casual required every other night in the main dining room.
Wine Corkage 1 bottle per person allowed on embarkation day. $35 corkage fee if consumed in a dining venue.
Last Night Bags Leave bags outside your stateroom the night before disembarkation. Keep an overnight bag for last morning essentials.
